01 — About

An electrician who treats every panel like a signature.

I started on building sites in Dhaka in 2017, learning complete electrical installation — conduit, cable tray, DB and main-panel work. From there I moved to overhead LV and MV line work with Patuakhali Palli Bidyut, then into industrial utility at Rapid Design Ltd., keeping production lines and motors running.

Today I'm a Senior Electrician at STIFFTEC International, installing and connecting the distribution boards that power boilers, booster pumps, hot-water pumps, air compressors and industrial washing lines.

A simple circuit, from call to handover.

    Tell me the job
